# Code of Conduct

Contributors must communicate respectfully, review technical claims against evidence, and make participation safe regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the project. Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer. Reports will be handled with discretion and without retaliation.
